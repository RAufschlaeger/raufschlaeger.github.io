I am a Research Assistant and Doctoral Student at the **Deggendorf Institute of
Technology (THD)**, based at the **Institute ISACS**. I have a background in
Mathematics and Computer Science, and my work sits at the intersection of
privacy and machine learning — AI for Cyber-Security and Cyber-Security for AI
— with a focus on making data useful while keeping individuals protected. I
started my PhD studies in 2024.

Since March 2024, I have also been a freelance lecturer for the "AI Project"
course in the Artificial Intelligence Bachelor's program at THD, teaching the
course each semester from 2024 to 2026.

Previously:

- **Sept–Nov 2022:** Teacher trainee (Referendariat) in Computer Science and
  Mathematics at the Erasmus-Grasser-Gymnasium, Munich.
- **May 2021 – Aug 2022:** Research Assistant at the **University of Passau**
  (FORWISS), on a joint project on model-based AI for improved sensor systems in
  3D metrology.
- **Dec 2018 – Mar 2021:** Student Research Assistant at the **University of
  Passau** (FORWISS).
- **Aug 2017 – Mar 2018:** Working Student (Full-Stack Web Development) at
  **msg**, Passau.
